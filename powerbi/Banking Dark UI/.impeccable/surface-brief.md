# Dark banking report

Mode: Operate. Scope: all report screens. The user has confirmed that redundant visuals may be simplified, including reducing pages, provided linked visuals across pages are preserved.

## Direction contract

THESIS: One navigable portfolio workspace; consolidate the overlapping overview and summary instead of repeating the same metrics and displaying unrelated measures as parts of one donut.

OWN-WORLD: Charcoal canvas, blue-tinted raised surfaces, ivory primary type, cool legible secondary text; teal lending, blue deposits, amber screening and violet fee accents. Native Segoe UI for portable Power BI rendering.

STORY: Read portfolio scale, select a segment, move to lending/deposits/screening/acquisition/fees, compare categories, and inspect native tooltips.

FIRST VIEWPORT: 1440-by-900 canvas, 184-pixel navigation rail, title and concise description, right-aligned native filters, KPI band and two aligned chart rows. Current page is highlighted; filters sit above the content they scope.

FORM: User-pinned dark working dashboard; code-led native report, not a marketing page. GPT Taste's wide type, dense complete grids, restraint and contrast are adapted to Power BI. GSAP, image heroes, AIDA pricing footers and random decorative component selections are not compatible with this platform.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
